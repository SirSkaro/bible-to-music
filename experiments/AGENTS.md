# Working with listening experiments

Read the [experiment design](README.md) when changing a case or interpreting a run.

- Keep the human answer key and the intended text separate from the first transcription request. Use neutral take IDs so filenames do not reveal the expected defect.
- Preserve the link from a finding to its source take and time window. Describe a missing spoken or sung word as an inference when the neighboring words provide the timing.
- Distinguish an observation about the mix from one about an isolated vocal. The mix is what the producer and eventual listener will hear.
- Use small synthetic examples for application tests. A claim about the quality of a listener needs a recorded take and a human judgment. Report misses and false alarms alongside successful detections.
- Treat the [producer QA worksheet](producer-qa.md) as a versioned source of criteria. Record uncertainties as audition requests. A model's plausible explanation is evidence to check. Only the producer can supply the missing human label.
